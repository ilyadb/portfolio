Cultivating trust at Moe Leto

    Designing a digital presence for one of Russia's largest greenhouse producers.

    I joined the project to redesign Moe Leto's corporate website and create a digital experience that better reflected the scale and quality of the business.

    We set out to bring together product information, business communications, company content, and brand storytelling into a single cohesive experience.

    By simplifying navigation and structuring content around user needs, we created a website that helps different audiences find relevant information while reinforcing trust in the brand.
