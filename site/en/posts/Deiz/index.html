Unifying car services at Deiz

    Creating a digital service for one of Dubai's premium automotive dealerships.

    Deiz served Dubai's affluent community through Instagram and personal relationships but faced growth constraints from entirely manual processes. Every inquiry required multiple touchpoints, creating bottlenecks that frustrated clients and overwhelmed staff.

    My work touched designing a easy application process for multiple B2B & B2C servcies and products (rent, buying, maintanance, tuning etc.) for one of the biggest premium dealership in Dubai.

    Disciplines
    Design engineering Research Human interface design
    Duration
    Early 2025 - 2026
    Role
    Full-time designer Part-time everything else

    Discovery

    First of all, I had to find the main pain points in User Journey and validate them. I was lucky to have access to the user support team, and their insights were really helpful.

    In order to validate them, I conducted several short interviews with Deiz current and potential clients. It helped to get the list of validated pain points. To figure out all the obstacles and missing points, I prepared the CJM of some sort with the whole process of getting a loan. It helped.


    Quick summary, staff were spending hours responding to TikTok and Instagram messages, manually checking availability, and repeating the same information to every new inquiry. This created high operational costs while delivering a slow, inconsistent experience.


    After testing and refining with team the flow multiple times, I smoothed out the rental process so clients can book and pick up cars with minimal friction, while keeping all verification steps secure.

    Solution

    An all-in-one hub for everything car rental-related, where users can view all current bookings and their details, reserve new vehicles, or check personalized offers, with bookings confirmed in as little as 5 minutes.
